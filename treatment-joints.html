Why Painkillers Accelerate Joint Degeneration

When knee or spine cartilage begins to wear down, pain is the body’s protective biological brake telling you not to overstress the joint. Conventional management relies heavily on NSAIDs (Aceclofenac, Diclofenac) and intra-articular steroid injections. While they numb the pain temporarily, they mask joint friction, causing patients to continue grinding degraded articular surfaces. Furthermore, chronic NSAID usage is the number one medical cause of gastric ulcers and drug-induced renal impairment.

Classical homeopathy takes a restorative physiological approach. By selecting remedies that match the patient's exact modalities — such as pain worse on beginning motion but better on continued movement (Rhus Toxicodendron), pain aggravated by slightest touch or movement (Bryonia Alba), or bone-deep aching in damp cold weather (Calcarea Fluorica, Ruta Graveolens, Ledum Palustre) — Dr. Srinivasan halts inflammatory erosion, optimizes periosteal circulation, and preserves joint independence.

Managing Disc Bulges & Sciatic Nerve Compression

In Lumbar (L4-L5, L5-S1) and Cervical (C5-C6, C6-C7) disc bulges, mechanical nerve impingement causes shooting lancinating pains, tingling, numbness, and burning down the legs or arms. Homeopathic neuro-musculoskeletal medicines reduce the inflammatory perineural edema surrounding nerve roots, eliminating the radiating agony without spine surgery.

Joint & Spinal Disorders Successfully Treated

  • Knee Osteoarthritis: Crepitus (cracking sounds), difficulty climbing stairs, squatting pain, cartilage wear.
  • Cervical Spondylosis: Neck stiffness, dizziness, numbness tingling into fingers, shoulder-blade spasm.
  • Lumbar Spondylosis & Sciatica: Lower back catch, radiating shooting shock down back of thigh and calf.
