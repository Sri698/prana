Our Ethical Medical Stance: True Integrative Support

At Prana Homeo Care, we maintain strict medical integrity. We do not claim to replace primary oncology treatments like surgery, chemotherapy, or radiotherapy. Instead, we work synergistically alongside your oncologists to protect vital organs, counteract treatment toxicity, alleviate suffering, and maximize Quality of Life (QoL).

Holistic Palliative Framework

The 6 Pillars of Palliative Cancer Support

Carefully personalized to each patient’s current stage, treatment phase, and constitutional vitality.

Treatment Protection

Chemotherapy Side-Effect Relief

Controls intractable nausea, persistent vomiting, metallic chemical taste in the mouth, and severe oral mucositis (mouth ulcers). Remedies like Cadmium Sulph and Ipecac provide immediate comfort.

Tissue Repair

Radiation Dermatitis & Burns

Heals hyperpigmented, burning, and blistered skin caused by radiation therapy. Ultra-diluted remedies like Radium Bromatum and Calendula promote rapid cellular regeneration and prevent deep fibrosis.

Neurological Comfort

Chemo Peripheral Neuropathy (CIPN)

Alleviates burning, tingling, numbness, and electric-shock sensations in the fingers, hands, and feet caused by taxane and platinum-based chemotherapy drugs.

Gentle Analgesia

Cancer Pain & Bone Discomfort

Non-sedative constitutional relief for deep visceral aches, post-operative nerve pain, and metastatic bone pain. Helps patients reduce excessive narcotic and opioid dependence safely.

Vitality Regeneration

Cancer Fatigue & Appetite Restoration

Combats debilitating cancer-related fatigue (CRF) and muscle wasting (cachexia). Gently stimulates natural appetite, restores digestive digestive enzyme secretion, and supports red blood cell production.

Mind & Spirit

Emotional Calm, Sleep & Hospice Care

Dispels death anxiety, panic attacks, depressive thoughts, and restless sleeplessness. In advanced Stage IV hospice situations, ensures serene breathing, comfort, and dignified peace.

Zero Physical Strain
